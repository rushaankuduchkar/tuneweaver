A tactile melodic sketchpad for fleeting musical ideas.

TuneWeaver 2.0 bridges spontaneous vocal humming and studio production. Capture an off-the-cuff melody into your browser, map its pitch contour and rhythm onto expressive acoustic or analog instruments, and export DAW-ready stems in seconds.
